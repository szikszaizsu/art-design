Fine Art

Every Material, One Truth.

What I touch comes alive — material becomes feeling, feeling becomes form.

Bead Weaving

Tiny beads, endless patience. Every piece begins with a thought — a color, a pattern, a feeling I want to hold onto. That's where the work starts: bead by bead, row by row, through hours, sometimes days, until the pattern in my mind becomes reality under my hands. This isn't decoration — it's meditation. There's no room for rushing here, only presence. Every piece carries the state it was born in: the patience, the attention, the silence. Anyone can learn the technique — but the seeing behind it can't be taught.

Egg Decorating

A blank surface, a kesice, a drop of wax — and slowly, a centuries-old pattern comes to life. Wax-technique egg decorating isn't just decoration — it's a tradition I carry forward with my own hands. Every line is a decision, every pattern a story — shapes repeated for generations, passed down through time. This isn't fast work: applying the wax takes the same patience as bead weaving — line by line, shape by shape. The result is fragile, yet lasting: an object that carries within it the silence and attention it was born from.
