Op. 08 — Questions

Before you book

The things couples ask most. Anything else, just write.

Ask a question
How far in advance should we book?

Summer Saturdays go first, so six to nine months ahead is safe. For a weekday or an autumn date, three months is usually enough. Write even if your date is close.

Can you learn a song that isn’t on your list?

Yes. Send the title or a voice note. I arrange it for the group and play it in the call before the day, so you hear it first.

What do you need from the venue?

A flat spot about three metres wide, shade or cover outdoors, and a power point for the PA. I check the rest with your venue directly.

Do you travel outside the South West?

Yes, anywhere in the UK. Travel and a hotel night are added to the quote when the venue is more than two hours from Bath.

How do we pay?

A 30% deposit holds the date, the rest is due two weeks before. Bank transfer, with an invoice for each payment.

What happens if it rains?

We move indoors or under cover, and we agree plan B on the planning call. The music does not stop and the fee does not change.

Tell me about your day.

Send a date and a venue. I reply within two days with availability, a quote and a few ideas for the music.
