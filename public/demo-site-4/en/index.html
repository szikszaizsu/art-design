Care for every age

From a child’s first check-up to grandad’s dentures. Every dentist on the team works in their own speciality.

A child’s first check-up

The first visit is mostly play: we count teeth and show them the little mirror and the chair that goes up. We only treat if it’s needed.

Sealants & fluoride

We seal permanent molars as soon as they come through, around age 6 and 12. About 10 minutes per tooth, no anaesthetic.

Orthodontics

Fixed braces or clear aligners, for children and adults. We recommend a first orthodontic check around age 7.

Cleaning

Scaling, Airflow and a professional polish every six months. Children learn to brush properly here, with plaque-disclosing tablets.

Implants & dentures

Implants, implant-supported work and removable dentures. We repair dentures the same day whenever possible.

Emergencies

Every day we keep two slots free for our patients with toothache, broken teeth or lost fillings and crowns.

Frequently asked questions

What parents ask us most often on the phone.

At what age should I bring my child?

At the first tooth, or by age 2 at the latest. The first check-up takes 15 minutes and is mostly getting to know the clinic.

Can I book the whole family on the same day?

Yes. Call us and tell us what each person needs. We book the appointments back to back, usually after 4 pm.

What if my little one cries or won’t open their mouth?

We stop, we never force it. We book another short getting-to-know-us visit, free of charge, and carry on from there.

Do you work with state insurance (CAS) for children?

Yes, we have a contract with CAS Sibiu for part of the treatment of under-18s. At reception we’ll tell you exactly what’s covered.

How long is a regular check-up?

20 to 30 minutes, with an X-ray if the dentist thinks it’s needed. Afterwards you get in writing what’s next and what it costs.
