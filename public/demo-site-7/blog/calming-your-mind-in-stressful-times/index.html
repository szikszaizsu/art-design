A Simple Evening Skin Routine

Three steps, five minutes. A night routine you can keep up even on busy days.

A good routine does not need ten steps. This guide covers the basics: what to use, when to use it and how to build up over time. Whether you are starting fresh or simplifying what you already have, you will find something useful here.


Signs your routine needs a change


To choose the right products, start by looking at what your skin tells you.

Check your skin – Is it dry, tight, dull or uneven? Note how it looks in the morning and in the evening. Keep it simple – Change one product at a time so you can see what works. Ask for advice – Talk to a dermatologist for guidance that fits your skin.


A simple routine:


A clear order of steps makes any routine easier to follow.

Morning: Cleanse, apply a serum, then moisturizer and sunscreen. Evening: Cleanse, apply your treatment serum, then cream. Weekly: Add an extra step only when your skin is comfortable. Tip: Keep your routine short enough that you will use it every day.


Understanding Your Skin


Skin changes with age, weather and daily habits. Genetics play a part, and so do sun exposure, sleep and the products you use. Knowing what affects your skin makes it easier to choose what to put on it.


A plain plan works best. Start small: cleanse, treat and moisturize. Daily habits: sunscreen, enough water and enough sleep. Over time: add one new product at a time. Tip: Build the routine around products you enjoy using.


Habits That Help


Consistency matters more than the number of products.

Use sunscreen daily – It is the simplest way to protect your skin. Be patient – Most routines need a few weeks before you see a difference. Go gently – Introduce active products slowly and one at a time.
Example: Apply your night serum, wait a minute, then finish with cream. Keep the steps in the same order every evening.


Quick Tips


Small details make a routine work better.

Apply to clean skin – Products work best on freshly cleansed skin. Use a small amount – A few drops of serum are enough for the whole face. Do not forget the neck – Extend your routine below the jawline.
Recommended: The Orelia Daily Renewal Cream pairs well with any of our serums.


In Short


Good skin does not need a complicated routine. Pick a few products you like, use them regularly and give them time. Your skin will show the difference.

Common questions and simple answers:

"My skin feels dry." → "Add a richer cream at night."
"I want fewer lines." → "Use a retinol serum at night, slowly."
"My skin looks dull." → "Add a daily serum and sunscreen."
Tip: Ask our customer care team if you are unsure where to start.
