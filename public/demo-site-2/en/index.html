What men
ask us

Short answers to the questions we hear most.

I haven't seen a dentist in years. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most of our patients come after a long break. We start with a full check-up and tell you honestly what's urgent and what can wait, no finger-pointing.

How long does a visit take?

A consultation takes 30–40 minutes, a cleaning about 45. If you're on a tight schedule, tell us when booking and we'll fit in.

I smoke and drink a lot of coffee. Can anything be done?

Yes. A professional cleaning removes most stains, and if you want more, whitening takes your teeth a few shades brighter. We'll tell you honestly what to expect.

I grind my teeth at night. What can I do?

A night guard made from your impression protects your teeth and relaxes your jaw. It takes two short visits.

Will I know the price before we start?

Yes, always. You get the plan in writing, with stages and costs, and treatment only starts once you agree.
