Are you contracted with the national health fund?

Yes. The clinic has a contract with the Romanian health insurance fund (CAS), so some treatments are partly or fully covered. At your first visit we’ll tell you exactly what applies to you.

Does orthodontic treatment hurt?

It doesn’t hurt, but the first days after fitting or after a check-up can feel a bit tight. That’s normal, it passes quickly, and we’ll show you how to ease it.

When should I bring my child?

We recommend a first orthodontic check-up around age 7. It doesn’t mean braces right away. We just catch early the things that are easier to fix while children are still growing.

Braces or clear aligners?

It depends on your case. Aligners are discreet and removable, braces are very precise for complex corrections. At the consultation we show you both, with a preview of the result.

How long does treatment take?

Most treatments take 12 to 24 months. You get a clear written estimate before we start.

What happens at the first consultation?

We talk about what bothers you, do the exam and any X-rays needed, and walk you through the options. By the end you know your options and can decide in your own time.
