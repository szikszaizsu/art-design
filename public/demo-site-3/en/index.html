FAQ

What nervous
patients ask us

ACDr. Ana Ciobanu answers

I’m really scared. Can I really come just to talk?

AC

Yes. The first visit is a conversation and a visual check, with no instruments and no treatment. You go home with a plan and decide if and when we continue.

What is conscious sedation?

AC

A medicine that relaxes you deeply, given under supervision. You’re awake and can respond, but you don’t feel anxious and remember very little of the treatment.

Does the injection hurt?

AC

We use computer-controlled anaesthesia that delivers the solution very slowly. We apply a numbing gel first, so you barely feel it.

Can I stop the treatment at any time?

AC

Any time. We agree on a signal at the start, usually a raised hand, and stop straight away every time.

Will I know the price before we start?

AC

Yes. You get the plan in writing, with stages and costs, and treatment only starts once you agree.
